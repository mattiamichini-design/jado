# Extended UX traceability

For a single artifact change, use `jado-ux-artifacts`.

1. Read [the traceability contract](references/artifact-contract.md), the existing dossier, and relevant sources. The coordinator or assigned author can produce the dossier; delegate only independent work or required review with exclusive ownership and an assigned budget. Complete when the baseline scope, canonical artifacts, and writer are identified.
2. Distinguish sources, decisions, assumptions, and gaps. Link canonical artifacts in the four collections. Record why an artifact or relation is inapplicable instead of inventing a persona or journey to fill a matrix. Missing decisions block only dependent work. Complete when each promised outcome has traceability or an explicit gap.
3. Check IDs, links, outcome coverage, and contradictions. Reread files, keep the same author for corrections, and preserve the budget. Deliver the dossier index, verified coverage, sources, and open decisions through `jado-evidence-contract`. Documentary verification does not establish user-test results. Complete when every required relationship is verified or its limitation is recorded.
