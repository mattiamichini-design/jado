## Purpose

Understand how other solutions address the same need, what value they communicate and which choices can inform the project. This procedure is a proposal: comparison breadth and criteria depend on the question.

## Procedure

1. Define the audience, need and scenario to compare. Include direct alternatives and different ways to solve the same problem.
2. Use `jado-researcher` for official sources and documentation. Record links, dates and what you could verify.
3. Assign [reference research](/docs/jado/visual-research) to the visual researcher; use `mobbin-search` for relevant patterns and paths. A screenshot documents an interface choice, not its effectiveness with users.
4. Compare the same scenario: promise, entry, information hierarchy, steps, copy, error states and recovery. Mark unobservable parts.
5. Separate observation, interpretation and opportunity. Check differences against your audience’s needs.
6. Bring results into the [marketing context](/docs/definition/messaging) and [design alternatives](/docs/experience/explore).

## Comparison matrix

| Solution and source | Observed scenario | UI or message choice | Evidence limitation | Project hypothesis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Link and date | Same task for each solution | Verifiable description | What was not observed | Question to test |

## Completion criterion

Each opportunity is linked to a need and source. Choices do not derive solely from taste or a competitor’s popularity; the comparison prepares hypotheses to validate in your own context.
