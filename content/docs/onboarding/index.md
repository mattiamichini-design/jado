## First day

Get access to the necessary repositories. Start the playbook, read the [operating model](/docs/operating-model/end-to-end) and identify sources of truth. Open a real UX record and find the link from a story to its validation scenario.

**Outcome:** you know where to find the method, research, visual contract and components.

## First week

Start the product and Storybook following local READMEs. Examine a primitive, a pattern and a page. Open a UX Planner demo and a VariantHub comparison. After [setup](/docs/jado/configuration), open a new session in your AI coding tool and use `jado-run` for a scoped assignment. Read the [JADO route](/docs/jado) from entry to handoff. Open a role procedure and identify the conditions that activate its skills.

**Outcome:** you can choose the tool for the question and distinguish exploration from production.

## First prototype

Choose a focused design question with a stakeholder. Gather evidence and competitor references, clarify the message, connect stories and flows and prepare a prototype with documented components. Agree on a user test, synthesize observations and share the proposal and limitations. Any contribution through a PR follows team agreements.

## First design review

Read the contract, observe the path, record reproducible findings and separate UX risks from aesthetic preferences. Propose a change with an observable success criterion.

## Adapt it to the team

Each team sets its own onboarding contact, repository and tool access, schedule, first products and autonomy criteria. This route is a proposal based on the tools described here.
